Basmat Al Mawared Company Limited · Last updated 31 August 2026 · Applies to basmat-almawared.com

The short version. This website sets no advertising cookies and no cross-site tracking cookies. We do not sell or share your data with advertisers. The only things stored on your device are the ones listed in the table below, and everything that is not strictly necessary stays switched off until you agree to it.

1. What cookies and similar technologies are

A cookie is a small text file a website stores on your device. Related technologies such as local storage work the same way in practice: they keep a small amount of information in your browser between visits. Throughout this policy we use the word “cookies” to cover both, because what matters to you is what is stored and why, not the technical mechanism.

2. What this website actually stores

Below is the complete list. If something is not in this table, we do not store it.

Name Type Purpose Duration Category
bam_cookie_consent Local storage Remembers the cookie choice you made, so we do not ask again on every page. Contains only your preferences and the date you set them. 180 days Strictly necessary
bam_cv_draft_v1 Local storage Saves your CV Builder draft on your own device when you press Save Draft, so you do not lose your work. This never leaves your browser and is never sent to us unless you submit an application. Until you clear it Functional

No analytics are installed on this website at present. There is no Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel, no advertising tag and no heatmap tool. The Analytics option in our cookie settings exists so that if we ever add one, it cannot begin collecting anything until you have given permission. This policy will be updated and your consent requested again before that happens.

3. The categories explained

Strictly necessary

These make the website work and cannot be switched off. They remember your cookie choice and help protect our forms from automated abuse. They store nothing that identifies you personally and are never used for marketing.

Functional

These improve the experience but are not essential. Today this covers only the CV Builder draft saved on your own device. If you decline, the website still works fully — your CV draft simply is not kept between visits. Our company video is self-hosted and needs no consent.

Analytics

These would tell us which pages and vacancies are viewed most, so we can improve the site. As stated above, none are currently in use.

4. Third parties that may receive data

We keep third parties to an absolute minimum. Only one external service is involved in loading this website:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) — supplies the typefaces used across the site. Google receives your IP address as part of that request, which is unavoidable for any externally hosted font. Google Fonts does not set cookies.
  • Our company video is hosted on our own server, not on YouTube or any other platform. Playing it contacts nobody but us and sets no third-party cookies at all.

We do not use advertising networks, social media tracking pixels, data brokers, or any service that profiles you across other websites.

5. Your choices and how to change them

You were asked to choose when you first arrived, and declining was made exactly as easy as accepting. You can change your decision whenever you like:

  • Use the Cookie Settings link here or in the footer of any page to reopen the preferences panel.
  • Clear your browser's site data for this domain, which erases everything listed in the table and causes the banner to appear again.
  • Use your browser's own controls to block cookies generally. Every major browser offers this in its privacy settings. Blocking strictly necessary items may stop parts of the site working.

6. Your rights under Saudi law

We process personal data in line with the Kingdom's Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), administered by the Saudi Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority (SDAIA). Under the PDPL you have the right to:

  • be informed of the legal basis and purpose for collecting your personal data;
  • request access to the personal data we hold about you;
  • request that inaccurate or incomplete data be corrected;
  • request that your data be destroyed when it is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected for;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 8. We will respond within the period required by the PDPL. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may raise the matter with SDAIA.

7. Job applications and the CV Builder

This is worth stating plainly, because it is where the most sensitive information is involved.

  • The CV Builder runs entirely in your browser. Everything you type stays on your device. Nothing is transmitted to us, and we cannot see it. Pressing Download as PDF produces the file locally on your own computer.
  • An application is different. When you submit the form on our Apply page, the details and any CV you upload are sent to our recruitment team and stored securely for recruitment purposes. That is a deliberate action you take, and the form explains it before you submit.
  • Application data, including identity document numbers, is used only to assess your suitability for work and to complete the employment and visa formalities required in the Kingdom. It is not used for marketing and is not sold to anyone.
